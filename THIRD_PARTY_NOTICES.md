## Natural Earth

`vendor/ne_110m_land.geojson` and the land textures in `assets/` use
Natural Earth 1:110m land data. Made with
[Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/). The source data is
[public domain](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/).
Pinned to commit `ca96624a56bd078437bca8184e78163e5039ad19` of
`nvkelso/natural-earth-vector`. Original file hashes and paths for all vendored
dependencies are recorded in [vendor/sources.json](vendor/sources.json).
